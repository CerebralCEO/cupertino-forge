# App Clips (`[official]`)

- **Source:** https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips (updated June 9, 2025 for demo App Clips) `[official]`.
- **UI rules:** Linear, focused flow — no tab bars, complex navigation, settings, splash screens, ads, or WebViews; launch directly into the relevant task and allow completion without installing the full app `[official]`.
- **Auth & codes:** Defer account creation (prefer Sign in with Apple); generate App Clip Codes only via App Store Connect or Apple's CLI tool without altering them; print matte `>= 300 dpi` (`>= 600 ppi` SVG raster), Type 5 NFC `>= 35 mm`; always Title Case `"App Clips"` / `"App Clip Code"` `[official]`.
